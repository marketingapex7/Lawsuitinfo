<section id="overview">
<h2>What the Cook IVC filter lawsuit is about</h2>
<p>Cook IVC filter lawsuits allege defects in Cook filters and inadequate warnings about risks including fracture, migration, tilt, perforation, embolization, and difficult retrieval. Cook disputes the allegations. The <a href="https://www.jpml.uscourts.gov/sites/jpml/files/MDL-2570-Initial_Transfer-10-14.pdf">JPML transfer order</a> explains why federal cases were centralized for coordinated pretrial proceedings.</p>
<p>An imaging finding, adverse-event report, or unsuccessful retrieval does not by itself prove that a filter was defective or caused a compensable injury.</p>
</section>

<p><strong>For readers:</strong> A bellwether plan schedules representative case work; it does not decide the outcome or value of every case.</p>

<section id="filter-model-scope">
<span id="key-issues"></span>
<h2>Cook filter models and manufacturer boundaries</h2>
<p>FDA records identify Cook Celect, Celect Platinum, and Günther Tulip vena cava filters. The exact implant record controls. A note that says only “IVC filter” does not establish that the product was made by Cook.</p>
<p>Do not combine this litigation with claims involving Bard, Cordis, Boston Scientific, or another company. The manufacturer, model, catalog number, lot number, implant facility, and procedure date should be documented whenever possible.</p>
</section>

<section id="claimed-complications">
<h2>Claimed fracture, migration, perforation, tilt, and retrieval issues</h2>
<p>The MDL transfer order describes allegations that Cook filters were more likely to fracture, migrate, tilt, or perforate the vena cava. Individual records may instead show stable placement, a different medical event, or no injury requiring treatment.</p>
<p>Imaging should be read in context by qualified clinicians. Keep the original report, images when available, comparison studies, symptoms, and treatment decisions together.</p>
</section>

<section id="claim-review">
<span id="eligibility"></span><span id="claim-evaluation"></span><span id="harder-claims"></span>
<h2>Factors in an individual claim review</h2>
<p>A useful starting file identifies a Cook filter and documents the reason for implantation, follow-up, claimed complication, and any retrieval or treatment. Counsel still must assess defect theories, warnings, causation, medical need, alternative causes, damages, state law, and timing.</p>
<p>Include records showing clotting disorders, cancer, anticoagulation, trauma, later procedures, and other facts that may affect the medical analysis. Complete records are more useful than a selected timeline.</p>
</section>

<section id="settlement-status">
<h2>Cook IVC filter settlements and verdicts</h2>
<p>The <a href="https://www.insd.uscourts.gov/node/432">official MDL page</a> and reviewed orders do not announce an MDL-wide settlement program, universal compensation matrix, or payment deadline. A settlement or verdict in one case depends on that case's proof and does not establish a standard payment for others.</p>
</section>

<section id="records">
<h2>Device and imaging records to organize</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Product identification:</strong> device card, implant log, stickers, catalog, lot, serial, and facility response.</li>
<li><strong>Placement:</strong> procedure report, indication, consent, imaging, and discharge instructions.</li>
<li><strong>Follow-up:</strong> office notes, radiology reports and images, anticoagulation history, and retrieval discussions.</li>
<li><strong>Complication and treatment:</strong> emergency, specialist, retrieval, endovascular, surgery, pathology, and device-analysis records.</li>
<li><strong>Costs and impact:</strong> bills, insurance statements, travel, wage loss, and later care.</li>
</ul>
<p>Use the <a href="/lawsuits/cook-ivc-filter/device-records/">Cook IVC filter device-records guide</a> to organize model, imaging, retrieval, and expense evidence.</p>
</section>

<section id="cook-ivc-filter-deadlines">
<span id="state-law"></span>
<h2>What is the deadline to file?</h2>
<p>There is no single national deadline for every Cook IVC filter claim. State statutes of limitation and repose, implant and diagnosis dates, discovery facts, product identification, and prior filings can change the time available. MDL orders apply to particular filed cases and do not calculate a new claimant's deadline.</p>
</section>
