<section id="overview">
<h2>What the Roblox lawsuit is about</h2>
<p>Families have sued Roblox alleging that adults used the platform to find and groom children. According to the <a href="https://www.jpml.uscourts.gov/sites/jpml/files/MDL-3166-Transfer_Order-12-25.pdf">JPML transfer order</a>, the plaintiffs are minors or parents of minors who allege that predators contacted children on Roblox and then moved the conversations to other platforms such as Discord, Snapchat or Instagram, or to texts and video calls. The claims include negligence, fraudulent and negligent misrepresentation, failure to warn and design defect.</p>
<p>The order lists common questions about what Roblox knew about predators on the platform, whether its safety statements were false, and whether it could have used effective parental controls and age verification. Roblox, Discord, Snap and Meta dispute the allegations.</p>
</section>

<h2>Roblox lawsuit update</h2>
<p><strong>The arbitration fight comes first.</strong> A <a href="https://nighgoldenberg.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/MDL-3166-Roblox-Child-Sexual-Exploitation-Litigation-Order-Re-Schedule-and-Word-Limits-for-Motions-to-Compel-Arbitration-Docket-Entry-342-.pdf">May 18, 2026 order</a> set a fall 2026 briefing schedule for Roblox's and Discord's motions to compel arbitration in bellwether cases, with the hearing to be set by the court. If the court sends those claims to arbitration, they would leave the courtroom track. No ruling appears in the public record reviewed October 17, 2026.</p>
<p>The <a href="https://www.jpml.uscourts.gov/sites/jpml/files/Pending_MDL_Dockets_By_Actions_Pending-October-1-2026.pdf">October 1, 2026 JPML report</a> lists <strong>180 pending actions</strong> in <a href="/mdl/3166/">MDL-3166</a>, compared with 182 on September 1. Its 190 total historical actions include cases no longer pending.</p>

<section id="litigation-updates">
<h2>Verified litigation updates</h2>
<h3>May 18, 2026: arbitration briefing schedule</h3>
<p>The court set deadlines for bellwether motions to compel arbitration, oppositions and replies running from September through November 2026. (<a href="https://nighgoldenberg.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/MDL-3166-Roblox-Child-Sexual-Exploitation-Litigation-Order-Re-Schedule-and-Word-Limits-for-Motions-to-Compel-Arbitration-Docket-Entry-342-.pdf">order, Dkt. 342</a>)</p>
<h3>April 16, 2026: settlement master</h3>
<p>The court gave notice that it intends to appoint Thomas J. Perrelli to mediate settlement discussions and said the parties "should explore whether resolution is possible." He would not decide any issues in the case. (<a href="https://nighgoldenberg.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/MDL-3166-Roblox-Child-Sexual-Exploitation-Litigation-Notice-of-Intent-to-Appoint-Settlement-Master-Thomas-J-Perrelli-Docket-Entry-301-1-1.pdf">notice, Dkt. 301</a>)</p>
<h3>January 30, 2026: plaintiffs' leadership appointed</h3>
<p>The court appointed plaintiffs' co-lead counsel. Later orders set up direct filing, guardians ad litem for minor plaintiffs and anonymous filing. (<a href="https://nighgoldenberg.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/MDL-3166-Roblox-Child-Sexual-Exploitation-Litigation-Order-Appointing-Plaintiffs-Co-Lead-Counsel-and-Directing-Parties-to-Meet-and-Confer-Docket-Entry-137-.pdf">order, Dkt. 137</a>)</p>
<h3>December 12, 2025: MDL created</h3>
<p>The JPML centralized 31 actions from 12 districts, out of nearly 80 related actions across 18 districts. All four companies opposed centralization. The Panel noted likely defenses under Section 230 of the Communications Decency Act and the First Amendment, disputes over whether the companies owed a duty to protect users from third parties, and whether the Ending Forced Arbitration of Sexual Assault and Sexual Harassment Act applies. (<a href="https://www.jpml.uscourts.gov/sites/jpml/files/MDL-3166-Transfer_Order-12-25.pdf">transfer order</a>)</p>

<section id="eligibility">
<h2>Who may be affected</h2>
<p>The cases involve children who were contacted by an adult on Roblox and then groomed, sexually exploited, coerced into sending images, extorted, or assaulted, sometimes after the contact moved to another app. Parents usually bring the case for a minor child, and courts in this MDL allow minors to proceed anonymously.</p>
<p>Many states pause filing deadlines while the injured person is a minor and have extended deadlines for childhood sexual abuse. Because the rules vary widely, timing should be reviewed early.</p>
<p>This is separate from cases claiming that games or apps are addictive by design. Those claims are tracked on the <a href="/lawsuits/social-media/">social media addiction lawsuit guide</a>.</p>
</section>
