<section id="overview">
<h2>What the Lyft sexual assault lawsuit is about</h2>
<p>Riders have sued Lyft alleging that drivers sexually assaulted or harassed them because Lyft failed to put adequate safety measures in place. The <a href="https://www.jpml.uscourts.gov/sites/jpml/files/MDL-3171-Transfer_Order-1-26.pdf">JPML transfer order</a> lists the claims as negligence, misrepresentation, breach of contract, strict product liability and failure to warn. It describes common questions about whether Lyft failed to screen and train drivers, respond to complaints, change the app's safety design, and adopt video and audio monitoring. Lyft disputes the allegations.</p>
<p>A larger, separate federal MDL covers Uber. See the <a href="/lawsuits/uber-sexual-assault/">Uber sexual assault lawsuit guide</a> for bellwether trial results there.</p>
</section>

<section id="latest-update">
<span id="status"></span><span id="litigation-updates"></span>
<h2>Lyft sexual assault lawsuit update</h2>
<p><strong>The federal docket nearly doubled in two months.</strong> The <a href="https://www.jpml.uscourts.gov/sites/jpml/files/Pending_MDL_Dockets_By_Actions_Pending-October-1-2026.pdf">October 1, 2026 JPML report</a> lists <strong>134 pending actions</strong> in <a href="/mdl/3171/">MDL-3171</a>, up from 106 on September 1 and 72 on August 3. Its 148 total historical actions include cases no longer pending.</p>
<p>The MDL is less than a year old. No bellwether trial date or settlement program was identified in records reviewed October 17, 2026.</p>
<h3>February 5, 2026: MDL created over Lyft's objection</h3>
<p>The JPML centralized 17 actions from 10 districts before Judge Rita F. Lin. Lyft argued that the claims belonged in the California state coordinated proceeding, JCCP No. 5061. The Panel disagreed, saying it "cannot require these or any future plaintiffs to file in California state court." It chose San Francisco partly because Lyft is headquartered there and because that would help coordination with the state cases.</p>

<section id="eligibility">
<h2>Who may be affected</h2>
<p>The cases involve riders who report being sexually assaulted, groped, or harassed by a driver during a ride arranged through Lyft. Claim review usually looks at the trip record, what happened, how and when it was reported, any injuries and treatment, and the state where it happened. Filing deadlines vary by state, and several states have special rules for sexual assault claims.</p>
