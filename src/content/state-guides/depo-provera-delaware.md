<section id="state-overview">
<h2>What Delaware residents should know</h2>
<p>Delaware is one of the principal state-court venues in the Depo-Provera litigation. Federal claims are separately centralized in <strong>MDL-3140</strong> in the Northern District of Florida, where Judge M. Casey Rodgers coordinates common pretrial issues.</p>
<p>State and federal coordination does not turn the litigation into a class action. Each plaintiff must establish product use, injury, causation, timeliness, and damages.</p>
</section>

<section id="state-depo-provera-context">
<h2>Why is Delaware important in the Depo-Provera litigation?</h2>
<p>Case Management Order No. 11 reports that Delaware Judge Kathleen Vavala joined the federal MDL court and New York court for a joint conference on March 6, 2026. At that time, counsel reported 287 Delaware cases involving 405 plaintiffs, the largest state-court group listed in the order. The courts coordinate selected scheduling and discovery matters while the Delaware and federal cases remain separate.</p>
